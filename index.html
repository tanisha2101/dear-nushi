Dear Nushi,

There are people who quietly become home.

You became mine.

If someone asked me what love feels like, I wouldn't just describe the grand gestures. I'd tell them about a girl who woke up early just to wish me luck before my big days. A girl who somehow knows exactly how to make my difficult days lighter. A girl who calls me Bui. A girl who became my favourite person.

Today isn't just Girlfriend's Day. It's simply another reason to tell you how grateful I am that you exist.

Chapter One

It Started At Xavier's

My heart noticed you before I even understood why. Neither of us knew it then, but our forever story had already begun

Chapter Two

Gangtok Kitchen

Our first date. Good food. Endless conversations. And unknowingly... the beginning of my favourite memories.

Chapter Three

The Girl I Admire

Your kindness. Your heart. Your strength. Watching you become a teacher fills me with pride every single day. I will always admire the person you are.

Chapter Four

The Smile I Fell For

Your smile has this beautiful habit of making every bad day disappear. I still blush when you look at me. I hope I never stop.

Chapter Five

My Safe Place

Whenever you rest your head on my chest, the world suddenly becomes quiet. Home has never been a place. Home has always been you.

Chapter Six

My Favourite Person

My favourite everything is you. Not one part of you — all of it. Your smile. Your eyes. Your kindness. Your laughter. Every bit of you has become my favourite part of being alive.

Chapter Seven

My Peace

Before you, I never knew that a person could feel like peace. At the end of the day, just knowing you're there is enough to make everything feel good in my life always.

Chapter Eight

The Little Things

I love the way you call me "Dino." I love how your face lights up when you smile. I love the little kisses on my cheeks. I love how you rest your head on my chest as if that's where you belong. It's always been the little things that makes me fall for you again and again.

Chapter Nine

Every Tomorrow

Whatever tomorrow looks like, I hope you're smiling. I hope you achieve every dream you've ever worked for. And I wish life is kind enough, So that I get to stand beside you through every chapter that's still waiting to be written.

Final Chapter

Home

People spend their whole lives searching for a place where they truly belong. I found mine in you. Thank you for every smile, every hug, every moment, and every dream we've begun to build together. Happy Girlfriend's Day, Nushi. You'll always be my home. ❤️
